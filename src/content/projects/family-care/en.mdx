## Problem

Family-care features bring together connected monitoring, location awareness, and activity insights. The web experience needed to work within a native iOS application, including the onboarding and location interactions that connect these capabilities to the user.

## Approach

I served as Tech Lead for Family Care for iOS while contributing hands-on development to onboarding and location features. The implementation combined TypeScript, LitElement, Redux Toolkit, WebView integration, and REST APIs.

My responsibilities included technical coordination, code reviews, and supporting the team through multiple delivery milestones. The engineering work connected the web interface with the native iOS environment while keeping platform-specific constraints in view.

## Impact

I supported the on-schedule delivery of two major releases. My contributions combined the implementation of onboarding and location features with technical ownership and collaborative implementation reviews.
