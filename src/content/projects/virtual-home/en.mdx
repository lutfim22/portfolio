## Problem

Connected-device simulations need to account for the capabilities and navigation of the environment in which they run. Virtual Home brought smart-home devices and interactions to mobile, Family Hub, and TV experiences.

## Approach

I developed cross-platform web experiences for connected-device simulations and user interactions. The work involved adapting behavior to each platform’s capabilities and navigation requirements.

The technology stack included JavaScript, TypeScript, React, LitElement, Web Components, and WebView integration. My contribution focused on the web experience across the different native environments.

## Impact

My work contributed to connected-device simulation and interaction experiences across mobile, Family Hub, and TV. The contribution centered on making those interactions fit the requirements of each platform.
