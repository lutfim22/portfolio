## Problem

Air-quality monitoring and connected-device control bring together environmental data, APIs, and IoT devices. Air Care presents these capabilities through a web-based interface.

## Approach

I developed features that integrated air-quality data, APIs, and connected IoT devices. The work supported environmental monitoring and device control through the web interface.

The stack included JavaScript, TypeScript, LitElement, REST APIs, IoT integration, and Chart.js. My contribution focused on connecting those sources and capabilities to the application’s user-facing features.

## Impact

My contributions supported air-quality monitoring and device-control features in Air Care. The work brought environmental data and connected-device capabilities into the web experience.
